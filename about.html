Who We Are

About AER Holdings

AER — like air itself: essential, light, and free. We build companies rooted in that belief, giving people open access to better health and better living.

Our Story

Light. Free. Essential.

AER Holdings was founded in 2012 with a simple idea: that healthcare and everyday living should feel less heavy. Less bureaucratic, less gatekept, less complicated. Like air — accessible to everyone, without friction.

We started with EcoMed, building a primary care practice that removed the barriers between patients and the physicians who could help them. From there we launched BioVella to bring premium, personalized wellness within reach, and added RF Living to make owning a vacation property as effortless as it should be.

AER is not a passive investor. We work directly alongside our companies on operations, strategy, and growth — because we believe the best businesses are built by people who stay close to the work.
