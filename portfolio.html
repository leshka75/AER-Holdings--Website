EcoMed

Active

EcoMed is a modern primary care and urgent care practice built around the patient, not the insurance company. Founded to eliminate the friction, wait times, and impersonal care that define traditional healthcare, EcoMed delivers physician-led medical services that are accessible, efficient, and genuinely focused on outcomes.

EcoMed accepts both insurance and cash-pay patients, offering same-day and next-day appointments across a broad range of services — from routine checkups to acute care visits.

BioVella

Active

BioVella is a premium, physician-led wellness practice offering a curated menu of cash-pay services designed for patients who want more from their health. Rather than one-size-fits-all medicine, BioVella delivers personalized, proactive wellness — without insurance constraints.

BioVella's model is cash-pay only, enabling faster appointments, deeper physician relationships, and a clinical environment built entirely around the patient experience.

RF Living

Active

RF Living is a full-service vacation rental management company serving property owners and guests in the Sarasota, Florida area. RF Living handles every aspect of short-term rental management — from listing and marketing to guest services and property care — so owners can earn without the hassle.

RF Living's end-to-end model removes the complexity of short-term rental ownership, giving property owners passive income without the headaches, while ensuring every guest enjoys a consistently premium stay.
