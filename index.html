Our Mission

Investing where health meets
innovation

AER Holdings was founded on the belief that Americans deserve better access to high-quality, modern healthcare and wellness. We acquire, build, and operate companies that remove friction from the patient experience.

We are operators first. Each business in our portfolio benefits from shared infrastructure, strategic guidance, and a leadership team that has built and scaled health companies from the ground up.
